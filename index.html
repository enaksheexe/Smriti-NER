North Eastern Region

Keeping memories close, one gentle game at a time

স্মৃতি কাষতে থাকক

Smriti NER is a cognitive gaming and memory assistance platform for elderly people living with dementia — built around the languages, faces and places of North East India.

...

What the platform does

🧠

Adaptive brain games

Difficulty adjusts on its own from recent accuracy, so sessions stay encouraging instead of frustrating.

🖼️

Personal memory album

Family faces, places and stories kept in one calm place for reminiscence therapy at home.

📈

Caregiver insights

Simple trends across sessions so families and clinicians can spot changes early.

🔤

Rooted in the NER

Familiar culture Bihu, bamboo, Loktak, orange orchards with Assamese alongside English.

🛡️

Private by default

Everything stays on the family's own device. No accounts, no uploads, works on low bandwidth.

🤝

Made for gentle hands

Large type, big touch targets, high contrast, no timers that punish a slow answer.
